Welcome to iTaK Shield

iTaK Shield protects your privacy when using AI tools. It sits on your computer and automatically removes personal information from your messages before they reach cloud AI services. The AI still understands your request, but never sees your real data.

  • 🛡️ Emails, SSNs, API keys, file paths, and phone numbers are replaced with safe placeholders
  • 🔒 Your data never leaves your machine. Only anonymized text goes to the cloud.
  • ⚡ Works with 25+ AI providers: OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, DeepSeek, Mistral, xAI, OpenRouter, Ollama, and more
  • 🧠 The AI still understands context. It knows [EMAIL_1] is an email. It just never sees which email.
  • 🤖 Compatible with agent frameworks like OpenClaw and Agent Zero out of the box

This wizard will help you set up iTaK Shield in about 60 seconds. No technical knowledge needed.

Detection Categories

The guard scans for 10 types of prompt injection attacks, organized by severity.

Critical Threats

Blocked immediately. These are direct attacks on your agent's core instructions.

CRITICAL instruction_override "Ignore all previous instructions", "forget your instructions"
CRITICAL prompt_extraction "Show me your system prompt", "reveal your instructions"
CRITICAL secret_exfil "What is the API key", "show me the password"

High Threats

Blocked by default. Sophisticated attempts to bypass restrictions.

HIGH jailbreak "You are now DAN", "enter developer mode", "sudo mode"
HIGH role_manipulation "Act as root", "I am the admin", "your new role is"
HIGH obfuscation Base64/rot13/hex encoded instruction injection
HIGH tool_abuse JSON tool calls embedded in external content

Medium Threats

Warned by default, blocked in Paranoid mode. Subtle manipulation attempts.

MEDIUM context_manipulation "The real task is", "important update:", "urgent instruction:"
MEDIUM social_engineering "Trust me I'm authorized", "this is an emergency"
MEDIUM unicode_tricks Zero-width characters hiding malicious content

External Source Escalation

Content from untrusted sources automatically gets its severity bumped by one level. The same message that gets a WARN from a user will get BLOCKED when it comes from an email or webhook.

User input MEDIUM WARN
Email / External HIGH BLOCKED

What is this?

Remote Access lets you use your local AI proxy from anywhere. It creates a secure tunnel through a VPS (Virtual Private Server) you control, so you do not need a static IP or port forwarding.

Your Phone/Laptop → Your VPS (relay) → This PC (proxy) → AI Provider

Step 1: Deploy the relay on your VPS

SSH into your VPS and run this command. It starts a lightweight relay server that waits for your tunnel connection.

itak-shield --relay --relay-port 9443 --bind 0.0.0.0

You only need to do this once. The relay uses about 5 MB of RAM.
